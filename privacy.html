Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information Miura Media collects when you use the booking form on this site, and how that information is used.

What we collect

When you submit a booking inquiry, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • The service you're inquiring about
  • Your preferred date (if provided)
  • Any details you include in your message

We do not collect payment information, and we do not use tracking cookies beyond what is described below.

How we use it

Information submitted through the booking form is used solely to respond to your inquiry, discuss your project, and schedule sessions. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Booking submissions are processed through Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets) so that inquiries can be reviewed and responded to. Google's own privacy practices apply to that processing.

Your choices

You can request that we delete any information you've submitted by emailing us at mia@miuramedia.info. We will remove it from our records within a reasonable time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mia@miuramedia.info.
